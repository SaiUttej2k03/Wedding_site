Palace FAQs

How do we reach Jagmandir Island for the wedding ceremony?

Decorated solar ferries will depart continuously from The Leela Palace private jetty every 15 minutes starting at 03:45 PM on December 19. The boat ride takes approximately 8 scenic minutes across Lake Pichola.

What is Udaipur's weather like in December?

Days are pleasantly warm and sunny (22°C - 26°C), while evenings by the open lake turn crisp and cool (10°C - 14°C). We recommend bringing pashmina shawls, royal bandhgalas, or evening wraps for nighttime festivities.

Can we request specific vegetarian or Jain meals?

Absolutely. All meal spreads feature dedicated pure vegetarian, Jain, and international counters. Kindly mention any specific dietary requirements in your RSVP form above.

Are children welcome to the celebrations?

Yes, your little ones are warmly welcomed! The hotel also provides supervised children’s play lounges during late-night events should you wish to take advantage of them.
